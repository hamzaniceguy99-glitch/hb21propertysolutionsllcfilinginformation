How we work

Photograph it, itemise it, let them choose

Families stall on inherited property for understandable reasons. Nobody knows whether to clear it first or fix it first, whether the code letter matters, or what any of it costs — and while that is unresolved, the grass grows and the fines accrue.

An itemised quote breaks that. Seeing clearance, repairs and compliance as separate lines with separate prices lets a family do the urgent part now and the rest later, instead of facing one intimidating number and doing nothing.

The other half is honesty about renovation. Grieving families are an easy audience for a long list of improvements, most of which never return their cost in the sale. Saying so means a smaller job, and it is the entire difference between helping and extracting.
